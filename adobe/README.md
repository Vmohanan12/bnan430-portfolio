Adobe Express – Data Storytelling

Completed: November 2, 2026
Analytics Firm: Mohanan Analytics
Client: Bear Down Bank
Executive: Jordan Ellis, VP of Card Growth

Project Overview
I created a one-page Adobe Express report recommending that Bear Down Bank target Texas for its first Wildcat Rewards credit card pilot. Texas has 68 eligible customers out of 159, representing a 42.8% eligibility rate compared with 20.4% overall.

Design Justification
I used a horizontal bar chart to compare eligibility rates across states and highlighted Texas to make the recommended market easy to identify. I simplified my previous Tableau dashboard by removing unrelated visuals and focusing on eligibility rates and eligible customer counts to help the executive make a decision.
